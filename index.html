What PageTap will not do

  • It will not use the cloud. There is no server anywhere in this app. Scanning, filters and OCR all run on your phone, and no document is ever uploaded.
  • It will not ask you to make an account. No sign-up, no login, no password, no email address — ever.
  • It will not track you. No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no per-install identifier. The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.
  • It will not promise perfect OCR. On-device text recognition is best effort — it can miss or misread words. The page image is always the record of what you scanned.

Questions

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. PageTap has no server. The camera capture, the filters, the OCR and the finished PDF are all handled on your iPhone, and the file stays in the app’s own storage until you tap Share and choose where it goes. There is no analytics or advertising SDK in the app either.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to, and no email address to hand over. Open the app and scan. A Premium subscription is handled by Apple through your existing Apple Account, so even that does not create a PageTap account.

What does OCR do?

It reads the text in your scanned page and stores it as an invisible layer underneath the image, so the words in the PDF can be searched and copied instead of being just a picture. It runs on the phone with Apple’s on-device text recognition, it is best effort, and it is a Premium feature — free documents save without it.

How does the free tier work?

5 free scans a day. A scan is one saved PDF, any number of pages, saved with a small "Scanned with PageTap" footer on each page, with your last 5 scans kept in the Documents list. No card is needed and nothing is charged unless you subscribe.

What is the footer on free scans?

A small "Scanned with PageTap" line printed in the corner of each page on a free-tier document. It never covers your content, and Premium removes it entirely.

How accurate is the OCR?

Best effort. On-device text recognition can miss or misread words, especially on handwriting or poor lighting. The searchable text layer is a convenience on top of the page image, which is always the actual record of what you scanned.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In the iPhone Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops any future charge. Refunds are handled by Apple under their policies.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple under their policies, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. PageTap is iPhone-only today.

Where do my scanned files actually live?

On your phone, in the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing leaves the device unless you tap Share and choose where it goes.
