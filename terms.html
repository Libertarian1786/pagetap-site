Last updated: 22 September 2026

What PageTap is

PageTap turns paper documents into PDFs using your iPhone's camera. It captures pages with the document camera, applies a filter, optionally adds a searchable text layer, and builds a PDF you can save and share — entirely on your device.

OCR is best effort

The searchable text layer is a best-effort convenience, not a guarantee. On-device text recognition may miss words, misread characters, or fail to recognize a page entirely, especially on handwriting, poor lighting, or low-contrast paper. The page image itself is always the record of what you scanned; the text layer only makes that image's content searchable and copyable when recognition succeeds. Do not rely on the text layer alone for anything where an exact transcription matters.

Free tier and subscriptions

PageTap offers a free tier — 5 scans per calendar day (a scan is one saved PDF document, of any number of pages), saved with a small "Scanned with PageTap" footer printed on each page, and your last 5 scans kept in the Documents list. PageTap also offers the following auto-renewable subscriptions through PageTap Premium:

  • PageTap Premium Weekly — $4.99 per week.
  • PageTap Premium Monthly — $9.99 per month, with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers.
  • PageTap Premium Yearly — $29.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers.

Premium removes the daily document limit and the footer entirely, turns on the searchable OCR text layer, and keeps your full document history.
